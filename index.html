Our mission

Ideas. Action. Community.

Inspired by the student-activism model of Turning Point USA’s Club America program, this local hub is designed to give students a place to learn, debate, organize, volunteer, and participate in civic life. TPUSA describes Club America as a high-school program focused on American principles, free thinking, and grassroots action.

🇺🇸

American Principles

Explore the Constitution, founding principles, individual liberty, equal protection, and civic responsibility.

🎤

Free Speech

Host respectful discussions, debates, speaker events, and opportunities to challenge ideas with evidence.

📈

Free Markets

Learn about entrepreneurship, property rights, capitalism, economic freedom, and fiscal responsibility.
